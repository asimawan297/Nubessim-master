1. Install your eSIM
Go to “My eSIMs” in the NubesSIM app and follow the steps to install your eSIM. We offer three installation methods, but we recommend direct installation if possible. Make sure you have a stable internet connection during setup.


2. Activate your package
Once your eSIM is installed, follow the steps to enable your network connection. Your data package will start automatically upon activation — most packages will connect to the best available network in your location. Some eSIMs may require manual activation, so please check the instructions for your package.


3. Manage your eSIM
After activation, you can easily track your data usage in the app. You’ll also receive notifications before your data runs out or your package expires — so you’ll always stay connected without surprises.


4. Top up your eSIM
Running low on data? No problem! You can top up directly through the NubesSIM app in just a few taps, or switch to another available package anytime.
